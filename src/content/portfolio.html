Swara Pull Toy

A sustainable musical toy that gives children physical ownership of individual notes. A child chooses a note block from the Sa–Re–Ga scale, places it in the drum, strikes it with the mallet and pulls the toy along.

Product designPlywood & HDPEPrototyping

Convertible furnitureErgonomicsMechanism design

Kosha

"Form embraces function." Kosha is a relaxing chair system whose outline is drawn from the geometry of a peanut shell, combined with ergonomic support, premium material craftsmanship and an angle-adjustable mechanism.

Peanut-shell formSculptural seatingErgonomic comfort
